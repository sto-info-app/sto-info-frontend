The Storytime content policy

Storytime is a place for Star Trek Online fan fiction written by the community. These are the rules everything published here has to meet. You are asked to confirm your Story meets them before you publish it, and anybody reading can report something they believe does not.

If something breaks these rules

Report it. A report goes to the site’s administrators and never removes anything by itself — somebody reads it and decides. You are not told what was decided about somebody else’s work, and the person you reported is never told who reported them.

If your work is removed

You keep it. Removed work stays in your own pages, flagged with the reason an administrator gave, so you can see exactly what was said. You may appeal once, and if the appeal is upheld the work goes back as it was.
