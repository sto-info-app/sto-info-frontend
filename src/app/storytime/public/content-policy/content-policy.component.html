1. Fan-created and unofficial

Storytime is for unofficial, non-commercial fan work. Nothing published here is official Star Trek or Star Trek Online content, and nothing may claim to be: not as canon, not as approved or endorsed by a rights holder, and not as a continuation or replacement for anything official.

Within that, build what you like. Your own continuity, Stories that connect to each other, Arcs written with other people — all of it is welcome, so long as it stays clearly identifiable as fan work.

2. Non-commercial

STO Info does not charge for Storytime and will not. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, paid memberships, premium Stories or paid Chapter access, and creators may not add any.

That rules out selling access, paid early access, affiliate marketing, sponsored placements, advertising within Stories and commercial solicitation. Published Stories stay freely readable here.

What you do professionally outside STO Info is your own business. The rule is about using this service to make money.

3. Who owns what

Star Trek, Star Trek Online, and every character, species, ship, location, organisation, design and mark that came with them belong to their rights holders. Publishing a Story here transfers none of that to you, to another member, or to STO Info — and using an established character in your Story does not make it yours.

Your original writing stays yours, as far as the law allows: your prose, dialogue, characters, plots, artwork and recordings. Publishing gives STO Info only the limited rights needed to run the service, set out in the Terms of Use. It does not let STO Info sell your work or put it behind a paywall.

Only submit material you made, have permission to use, or are otherwise entitled to use. That applies as much to artwork, music, recordings and screenshots as it does to writing. Crediting somebody is not the same as asking them.

A rating is not permission: an Adults Only label does not allow anything this policy otherwise prohibits, and explicit sexual images and video are never permitted whatever the rating says.

6. AI-generated writing

Storytime exists to show what the community writes. Stories, Chapters and other substantive narrative written by generative AI are not permitted, and AI-written prose must not be published as your own work.

AI must also not be used to imitate somebody’s voice, to fake audio or video of a real person, to impersonate another creator, to reproduce somebody else’s work, or to get around copyright and moderation rules.

Help with spelling, grammar, formatting and accessibility is not what this section is about. Writing the story for you is.

7. Real people

A character and the actor who plays them are not the same thing. Do not impersonate a real person, claim somebody contributed when they did not, put words or performances in their mouth, or use another member’s identity, voice or likeness without their agreement. If you credit a real person for a voice, performance or likeness, you need their permission to do it.

8. Media and links

Storytime is not a video host. Supported external media goes through the Storytime media controls, which at launch means YouTube, and Chapter text may not carry arbitrary external links. Anything that would break these rules if uploaded here breaks them when embedded from somewhere else, and disguised addresses, redirects and similar workarounds count as attempts to break them.

9. Reporting

Report anything you believe breaks these rules, infringes copyright, misuses your identity or work, or creates a safety concern. Report in good faith: knowingly false or abusive reports are themselves actionable.

A report goes to the site’s administrators and never removes anything by itself — somebody reads it and decides. You are not told what was decided about somebody else’s work, and the person you reported is never told who reported them.

10. Moderation and removal

An administrator may do nothing, require a warning or a corrected rating, hide media or comments, remove credits, unpublish, or mark content as Removed. Removed is a moderation state, separate from you deleting, archiving or unpublishing your own work.

Removed work stays stored, disappears from public listings, search, Spotlight, feeds and navigation, and its public address reports that it is gone rather than showing it.

If your work is removed

You keep it. Removed work stays in your own pages, flagged with the reason an administrator gave, so you can see exactly what was said. You may appeal once, and if the appeal is upheld the work goes back as it was. An appeal does not restore anything by itself.

11. Copyright complaints

If you believe something here infringes copyright you own or represent, tell STO Info. Say which work is affected, which Storytime content you mean and where it is, what your relationship to the work is, why you believe the use is unauthorised, and how you can be contacted. Content may be restricted while a complaint is looked at, and the creator may be given a chance to respond.
