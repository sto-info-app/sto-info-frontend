Unofficial and non-commercial

STO Storytime is an unofficial, non-commercial fan storytelling service run as part of STO Info. Nothing here is charged for, and nothing is behind a subscription or paywall.

The Stories, Chapters, Arcs and Characters published here are written by fans. Unless it explicitly says otherwise, Storytime content is not official Star Trek or Star Trek Online content, is not canon, was not produced or published by the owners or licensees of either, and is not endorsed, sponsored or approved by them.

What creators own

Creators bring their own original material: prose, dialogue, characters, plots, artwork and other creative work. So far as a creator is legally entitled to hold rights in those original contributions, those rights stay with the creator. Publishing through Storytime does not transfer them to STO Info.

Those rights still sit underneath the Star Trek and Star Trek Online rights the work is built on. Creating an original Starfleet officer may give you rights in your own creative contribution where the law allows — it does not give you Starfleet, the Federation, or Star Trek.

What STO Info is granted

Only what running the service requires, as set out in the Terms of Use: storing your content, processing and formatting it, showing it to readers, creating previews and excerpts, making it available through search, feeds and Spotlight, keeping backups, and moderating what is reported.

That licence does not permit STO Info to sell Storytime content, charge for access to it, or place it behind a subscription or paywall. Publishing here transfers no ownership.
