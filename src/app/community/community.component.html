Galactic Personnel Registry

A directory of officers who have chosen to make their record public. Find other officers, see the captains they command, and share your own record with the fleet.

Nothing appears here until you opt in from your profile, and your real name, email address and private notes are never shown.

Friends

Add officers you meet in the registry as friends, and keep them all in one place. A request has to be accepted before it becomes a friendship, so nobody joins your list uninvited.

You can also block an officer. Blocking ends any friendship between you, stops either of you sending the other a request, and hides your records from each other. The other officer is never told.
