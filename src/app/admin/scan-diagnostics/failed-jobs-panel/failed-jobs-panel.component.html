Failed jobs

Background jobs that ran out of attempts, queue by queue, newest failure first. Each shows internal IDs and a reason code only, never what the job carried or its error’s text. Retry sends a job round again when that could change something; Discard removes it. Each asks for a reason, kept in the Security Log.
