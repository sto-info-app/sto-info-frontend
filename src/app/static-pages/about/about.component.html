The App

This app was born from having my Excel spreadsheet getting too big to manage ninety-seven characters across seven accounts on three platforms! As my spreadsheet struggled to keep up, I made this app.

The plan is for this companion app to help you track your STO accounts, characters, and fleets. It will also help you track your reputations, duty officers, and more. The app will also provide you with a way to track your progress in the game and help you plan your next steps. Additionally, it will include feature updates that enhance gameplay and user engagement. Stay tuned for exciting new functionalities in future updates!

It is still a work in progress and will be released with minimal features, but I aim to add more features as soon as possible.
