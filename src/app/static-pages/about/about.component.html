About the {{ appTitle }}

The {{ appTitle }} was born from its creator's need to track his characters and accounts in a massive Excel spreadsheet. The problem was, there was so much data to manage that it was getting harder to manage his ninety-seven characters across seven accounts on three platforms!

His solution was to create the {{ appTitle }}.

The plan is for this companion app to help you track your STO accounts, characters, fleets, reputations, duty officers, statistics, and, eventually, a lot more.

This app will also provide you with a way to track your progress in Star Trek Online, share your data with friends, and help you plan your next steps. Additionally, it will include feature updates that enhance gameplay and user engagement. Stay tuned for exciting new functionalities in future updates!

The {{ appTitle }} is still a work in progress and its initial release has minimal features, but the creator aims to add more functionality as soon as possible... trust me, it will be installed on Tuesday!

Costs

The STO Info (Star Trek Online Info) application is a free, independent, fan-run project built for the community.

Patreon is not required to use the app, unlock features, or gain access to anything.

If you choose to support the project, you are helping cover infrastructure costs and the time needed to maintain and improve it — nothing more, nothing less.

Estimated costs during development are over £600 (~$800 USD) per year, with this expected to increase as the user base grows.

If you find the site useful and would like to help keep it running, you can choose to support it with a voluntary donation.

There are no paid features, no exclusive access, and no obligation to contribute. The project will remain free for everyone.

The only reward for supporting the project is the satisfaction of knowing you are helping to keep it going and your name will be added to the Supporters page (a single list; no amounts shown).
