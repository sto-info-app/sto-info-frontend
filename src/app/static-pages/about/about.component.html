About me, the developer

My name is Steve, mostly known in the Star Trek Online community as MidNiteShadow7. I have been a podcaster and I was previously a STO forum moderator. For my work, I am a software developer from the UK.

The App

This app was born from having my Excel spreadsheet getting too big to manage 96 character across 6 accounts on 2 platforms! As my spreadsheet was struggling to keep up, I decided to make an app.

The plan is to have this app be able to track informarion about your STO accounts, characters and fleets.
