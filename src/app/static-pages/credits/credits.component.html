LCARS Theme

Michael Okuda designed the LCARS (Library Computer Access and Retrieval System) graphical user interface for Star Trek: The Next Generation.

The Star Trek Online Info Portal web theme was initially taken from TheLCARS.com. The LCARS-Inspired Website Template was designed and developed by Jim Robertus and then adapted by Steve Roberts.
