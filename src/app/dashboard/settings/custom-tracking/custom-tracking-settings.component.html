What this is

Custom Tracking lets you decide what to record about your own STO accounts and characters. You define the sections, tabs and fields once, and then record a separate answer for each account or character.

Everything is private until you say otherwise. A field appears on your public registry page only when the field, the tab, the section and the record itself are all public.

The rules

Everything you enter here is yours and is your responsibility. Do not store other people's personal information, and do not publish anything you would not put on a public page — because that is what a public field is.

Read the Terms of Use. They apply to everything recorded here, and the content agreement you accepted sits alongside them.

What happens when you delete something

Deleting a section, tab or field takes the answers recorded against it too. Deleted definitions and answers are kept for 180 days and then removed for good, so a mistake can be put right by asking within that window — but not from this page.
